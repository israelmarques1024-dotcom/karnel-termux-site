# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our projects seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please do not open a public issue. Instead, please report it privately.

### How to report

Please send an email to **israelmarques1024@gmail.com** with a detailed description of the vulnerability and steps to reproduce it.

### What to expect

We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and provide a timeline for a fix. Once the vulnerability is resolved, we will credit you for the discovery (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).

Thank you for helping keep our community safe!
